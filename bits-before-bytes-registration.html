What presenting means

  • Pick a paper for its methodNot its field. Anyone in the room should be able to use it.
  • About 15 minutesThe idea and how it was done. The rest of the half hour is discussion.
  • No polish neededA few slides, no need to be perfect.

Paper ideas

  • LLMs as research tools
  • Text as data
  • Images, audio and video as data
  • ML for measurement
  • AI agents in the research workflow
  • Validation and reproducibility
